The attached presentation introduces the GhostField architecture and its capabilities for building and simulating social media environments at exercise scale. It explains how AESOP defines actors, relationships, narratives, and events; how SynSM generates social networks and interactions as events unfold; and how MyMuse allows participants to introduce content and influence subsequent developments. Examples from Island Shield illustrate the simulated networks and messaging alongside real-world data. The presentation also describes how Artemis and ORA PRO support analysis, how scenarios can be delivered through ION and SITE, and how records of analysts’ actions support performance evaluation and after-action review.
